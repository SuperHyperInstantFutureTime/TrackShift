What are splits?

Splits tell TrackShift how to split the profits of a product. For example: The Artist (50%), The Label (50%). They are only applied to profit, when all Costs have been recouped.

Splits always add up to 100%, with the label automatically allocated the remaining % from what is entered into the split.
