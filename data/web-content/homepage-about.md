## We are a startup based in Derby, UK.

### Richard ‘Biff’ Birkin 

Biff has been in bands since school, and spent his teens and twenties releasing and touring in the DIY scene in the UK and Europe. As well as running his own label he’s released on many different other labels.

### Greg Bowler 

Greg is a problem solver, most often using software as his primary tool. Greg has built large-scale fintech solutions and award-winning apps to improve ticket sale accessibility for live events and venues.
